# Pell & Arden CPAs — Engagement & Fee Guide

Fictional sample document for demonstration purposes. Internal guide for partners and managers quoting new work. Tax year 2025 season (filed in 2026).

## 1. Who We Serve

Pell & Arden serves owner-managed businesses with revenue under $25 million and their owners. Core services: business and individual tax returns, monthly bookkeeping and advisory, payroll oversight, and reviewed (not audited) financial statements. The firm does not perform audits of public companies or SEC filings.

## 2. Standard Fees

| Service | Starting fee |
|---|---|
| Individual return (Form 1040), W-2 income only | $450 |
| Individual return with Schedule C (sole proprietor) | $850 |
| Each rental property (Schedule E) | $175 per property |
| S corporation return (Form 1120-S) | $1,950 |
| Partnership / multi-member LLC return (Form 1065) | $2,150 |
| C corporation return (Form 1120) | $2,450 |
| Each additional state return | $225 |
| Monthly bookkeeping (up to 150 transactions) | $650 per month |
| Reviewed financial statements | from $6,500 |
| IRS notice response (simple, one notice) | $350 |

Rush fee: 25% surcharge for returns requested with fewer than 10 business days before the filing deadline. Fees for prior-year catch-up returns are quoted at 1.5 times the current fee.

## 3. Engagement Letters

- No work starts until the client has signed the engagement letter in SafeSend and paid the deposit.
- Deposit: 50% of the quoted fee for new clients; returning clients in good standing pay no deposit.
- Every engagement letter states the services, the tax years covered, the fee or fee basis, what the firm will not do (for example, it does not detect fraud), and the client's responsibility to provide complete and accurate records.
- Engagement letters are renewed every year; the firm never relies on a prior year's letter.

## 4. Client Acceptance

Before accepting a new client, the manager must:
- Confirm identity with a government-issued photo ID for individuals and formation documents for businesses.
- Ask for the last 2 years of filed returns and any open IRS or state notices.
- Run the firm's independence and conflict check (the firm will not prepare returns for both parties in an active divorce, or for two business partners in a dispute).
- Decline any prospect who asks the firm to take a position the manager believes lacks substantial authority, or who refuses to provide records.

## 5. Payment Terms

Invoices are due on receipt. Completed returns are released for e-filing only after the invoice is paid in full. Balances over 60 days past due pause all work for that client, including bookkeeping.

## 6. Scope Changes

If a client brings items not covered by the quote (for example, a sale of a business, a new rental property, foreign accounts, or cryptocurrency activity over 50 transactions), the manager sends a written scope change before doing the extra work. Cryptocurrency activity is billed at $95 per hour.
