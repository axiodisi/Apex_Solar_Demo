# Pell & Arden CPAs — Tax Season Client Procedures

Fictional sample document for demonstration purposes. Applies to the 2026 filing season (tax year 2025).

## 1. Internal Deadlines

The firm sets its own document deadlines ahead of the federal due dates so returns can be reviewed properly.

| Return type | Client documents due to the firm | If documents arrive later |
|---|---|---|
| S corporation and partnership returns | February 15 | Extension is filed automatically |
| Individual returns | March 15 | Extension is filed automatically |
| C corporation returns (calendar year) | March 15 | Extension is filed automatically |

Clients are told in writing that an extension extends the time to file, not the time to pay. Estimated tax still owed must be paid by the original due date to avoid penalties and interest; the firm calculates an extension payment for every extended client.

## 2. Document Checklist (Individuals)

- All W-2s, 1099s (NEC, MISC, INT, DIV, B, R, K), and K-1s.
- Form 1098 mortgage interest statements and property tax bills.
- Records of estimated tax payments made, with dates and amounts.
- For business owners: the year-end profit-and-loss statement and balance sheet, plus a mileage log if a vehicle is used for business.
- Charitable contribution receipts; a written acknowledgment from the charity is required for any single gift of $250 or more.
- Health insurance Form 1095-A if coverage was bought through the Marketplace.

## 3. Vehicle and Travel Expenses

The firm follows IRS Publication 463 for travel, gift, and car expenses. Clients who deduct business use of a car must keep a contemporaneous mileage log showing the date, destination, business purpose, and miles for each trip. Reconstructed logs prepared at tax time are flagged in review. Clients asking about business gifts are reminded that the deduction for business gifts is generally limited to $25 per recipient per year.

## 4. Record Keeping Advice to Clients

The firm's standard advice follows IRS Publication 583. Clients are told to keep records that support income, deductions, and credits until the period of limitations for that return runs out, and to keep employment tax records for at least 4 years. Records relating to property (purchase price, improvements, depreciation) should be kept until the period of limitations expires for the year the property is sold.

## 5. Secure Document Exchange

- Clients upload documents only through the SmartVault client portal. The firm never accepts tax documents by regular email.
- Staff must never request a full Social Security number by phone or email.
- Signed e-file authorizations (Form 8879) are collected through SafeSend; returns are not transmitted until the authorization is received.

## 6. Review Standard

Every return is prepared by one staff member and reviewed by a different CPA. Returns with any of the following require a second, partner-level review: a balance due over $25,000, a first-year S corporation, the sale of a business or rental property, or any foreign reporting.

## 7. IRS Notices

Clients forward any IRS or state notice through the portal within 5 days of receipt. The firm responds or requests more time before the response date on the notice. Notice responses are billed separately unless the notice resulted from a firm error, in which case there is no charge and any penalty caused by the firm's error is reimbursed.
