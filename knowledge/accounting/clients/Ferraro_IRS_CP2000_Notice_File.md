# IRS Notice File — CP2000 for Dale & Monica Ferraro, Tax Year 2024

Fictional sample document for demonstration purposes. Notice summary and response plan. Client: Dale and Monica Ferraro (Form 1040, married filing jointly). Responsible: Jonah Kwiatkowski (manager); approved by Margaret Arden (partner). Last updated October 16, 2026.

## The Notice

- Notice type: CP2000, Proposed changes to your 2024 Form 1040 (an automated underreporter notice comparing the return to information returns; it is not an audit and not a bill).
- Notice date: Monday, September 28, 2026. Client received it Thursday, October 1, 2026 and uploaded it to SmartVault on Friday, October 2, 2026 (within the firm's 5-day standard).
- **Response due date: Wednesday, October 28, 2026.** Internal target: mail and fax the response by Friday, October 23, 2026.

## Our Analysis

1. **$4,200 from Lakeside Property Group — disagree.** The payment was for drain work Brightwater Plumbing & Drain, Inc. performed (invoice BW-24-0917, paid November 14, 2024). It was deposited into Brightwater's business account and is included in Brightwater's 2024 gross receipts on its 1120-S. Lakeside issued the 1099-NEC under Monica's Social Security number instead of the company's EIN by mistake. Jonah asked Lakeside for a corrected 1099 on October 7, 2026; if it does not arrive before the response is sent, we will include the invoice, the bank deposit record, and the 1120-S page showing gross receipts.
2. **$312 from Harbor Point Bank — agree.** The 1099-INT was uploaded to SmartVault on March 3, 2025 but was missed in preparation. This is a firm error. The tax on $312 at the Ferraros' 24% bracket is about $75. Without the $4,200 item, the remaining understatement is too small for the accuracy-related penalty, so we expect the IRS to drop the $321 penalty and recompute interest on the $75 only.

## Response Plan

- Check the "I do not agree with some or all of the changes" box on the response form, sign as authorized under Form 2848 (on file, covers 2022–2026), and attach a cover letter, the supporting documents, and the agreed $312 item.
- Do not pay the $2,044 proposed amount. The Ferraros may pay the corrected tax on the $312 (about $75) when the IRS issues its revised notice.
- If the response cannot be completed by October 28, call the number on the notice to request more time before the due date.
