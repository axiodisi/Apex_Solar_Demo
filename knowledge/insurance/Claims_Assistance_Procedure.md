# Northgate Insurance Agency — Claims Assistance Procedure

Fictional sample document for demonstration purposes. How CSRs and producers help clients report and follow claims. Effective February 2026.

## 1. First Notice of Loss (FNOL)

- When a client calls about a loss, the CSR first confirms everyone is safe. For a fire, active water leak, or gas smell, the client is told to contact emergency services or the utility first.
- The CSR reports the claim to the carrier within 4 business hours of the client's call (same day for any injury or any loss over $25,000). If the carrier offers 24/7 direct reporting, the CSR gives the client the carrier's claims number as well.
- The claim number, adjuster name, and adjuster phone number are entered in the agency management system and emailed to the client.

## 2. Information to Collect

- Date, time, and location of the loss, and how it happened.
- Photos or video of the damage before cleanup, if safe.
- For auto: the other driver's name, insurer, policy number, plate number, and the police report number.
- For property: a list of damaged items; receipts if available.

## 3. Mitigation

Clients are told to take reasonable steps to prevent further damage (for example, tarping a roof or turning off the water) and to keep the receipts. Clients should not throw away damaged property until the adjuster has seen it or agrees in writing.

## 4. Flood Claims

- Flood losses are reported to the flood carrier (NFIP or private), not the homeowners carrier. Many clients have both; CSRs check the file before reporting.
- Clients are reminded that NFIP claims have their own proof-of-loss deadlines and procedures described in the policy; the CSR sends the client FEMA's NFIP Summary of Coverage and the claims section of the policy.

## 5. Follow-Up Standard

| Claim size | Agency check-in |
|---|---|
| Under $10,000 | 7 days after FNOL, then at closing |
| $10,000 – $100,000 | Every 7 days until the adjuster's estimate is received |
| Over $100,000 or any injury | Producer calls the client every 7 days; Agency Principal is informed |

## 6. Disputes

If a client disagrees with the carrier's decision, the agency may help the client understand the denial letter and the policy wording, and request the carrier's claim supervisor review. The agency does not give legal advice and does not represent the client against the carrier. Clients considering legal action or a public adjuster are told they may consult an attorney.

## 7. Claims and Renewals

Any open claim is flagged at renewal. Clients with 2 or more property claims in 3 years are reviewed by the producer for possible remarketing before the renewal date.
