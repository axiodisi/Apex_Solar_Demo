# Northgate Insurance Agency — Producer Handbook

Fictional sample document for demonstration purposes. For licensed producers and customer service representatives (CSRs). Independent agency writing personal lines and small commercial. Edition 2026.

## 1. Commissions

| Line | New business | Renewal |
|---|---|---|
| Personal auto and home | 50% of agency commission | 25% of agency commission |
| Small commercial (BOP, general liability, commercial auto) | 40% of agency commission | 20% of agency commission |
| Flood (NFIP and private) | 50% of agency commission | 25% of agency commission |
| Life and disability | 60% of agency commission | 10% of agency commission |

Commissions are paid on the 15th of the month after the agency receives payment from the carrier. If a policy cancels within the first 90 days, the producer's new-business commission is charged back.

## 2. Binding Authority

- Producers may bind only within the binding authority granted by each carrier, as listed in the Carrier Authority Grid in the agency management system.
- No coverage may be bound during a binding moratorium (for example, when a named storm is within the carrier's moratorium area). CSRs check the Moratorium Board before binding any property policy.
- Commercial policies with total insured values over $5 million require Agency Principal approval (Marcus Bell) before binding.

## 3. Quoting Standards

- Every homeowners quote must include a flood discussion, documented in the client file, even if the home is outside a high-risk flood zone. Standard homeowners policies do not cover flood.
- Personal auto quotes must offer uninsured/underinsured motorist limits equal to the bodily injury limits. If the client chooses lower limits, the client signs the rejection or selection form.
- Umbrella coverage is offered to every client with a home value over $500,000 or a teenage driver.

## 4. Flood Insurance Rules of Thumb

- New NFIP policies usually take effect 30 days after purchase. Exceptions exist (for example, when the policy is required in connection with a new loan). Producers must confirm the effective date with the carrier and never promise immediate coverage.
- Clients who need building coverage above the NFIP maximum are offered an excess flood policy.
- Explain to clients that the NFIP summary of coverage is not the policy; the policy controls.

## 5. Renewals

- Renewal review starts 60 days before expiration for personal lines and 90 days for commercial.
- Any renewal with a premium increase over 15% triggers a remarket to at least 2 other carriers.
- Clients receive a written renewal summary at least 30 days before expiration.

## 6. Errors & Omissions Prevention

- Every coverage recommendation the client declines is documented in the client file with the date.
- Coverage changes requested by phone are confirmed back to the client by email the same day.
- Producers never tell a client a loss "will be covered." They say the claim will be reported and the carrier makes the coverage decision.
