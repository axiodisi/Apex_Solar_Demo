# Renewal Remarket — Brookside Coffee Roasters LLC, December 1, 2026

Fictional sample document for demonstration purposes. Producer worksheet comparing renewal options. Insured: Brookside Coffee Roasters LLC (owner Natalie Brooks). Producer: Ethan Saldana. Account CSR: Hannah Kowalski. Last updated October 16, 2026.

## Timeline

- Policies expire December 1, 2026 (Tuesday).
- Commercial renewal review started Wednesday, September 2, 2026 (90 days before expiration).
- Cascade Mutual BOP renewal quote received Friday, September 18, 2026.
- Client review meeting: Wednesday, October 21, 2026, 2:00 p.m. at the café.
- Written renewal summary must reach the client at least 30 days before expiration. Thirty days before December 1 is Sunday, November 1, so Ethan will send it by Friday, October 30, 2026.
- Natalie's decision needed by Friday, November 13, 2026 so the new policies can be bound before December 1.

## Why the BOP Is Being Remarketed

Cascade Mutual's BOP renewal premium is $8,140, up $1,300 (19.0%) from the expiring $6,840. Cascade cited the open water claim (CM-26-118204) and a general property rate increase. Because the increase is over 15%, the BOP was remarketed to other carriers.

## BOP Quotes

All quotes: business personal property $350,000 (the client may revisit the $425,000 increase she declined last year), business income up to 12 months with 72-hour waiting period, general liability $1,000,000 / $2,000,000, and the landlord as additional insured.

| Carrier | Annual premium | Property deductible | Equipment breakdown | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cascade Mutual (renewal) | $8,140 | $2,500 | Included | Same terms as expiring; keeps the umbrella with Cascade |
| Harborview Specialty | $7,310 | $5,000 | Included | Lowest premium, but double the deductible |
| Summit Ridge Insurance | $7,595 | $2,500 | Not included; available by endorsement for $410 ($8,005 total) | Requires the umbrella to move to Summit Ridge |
| Lakemont Insurance | Declined to quote | — | — | Declined because of the open water claim |

## Umbrella

Cascade Mutual will only keep the $2,000,000 umbrella if it also writes the BOP. Summit Ridge quoted a matching $2,000,000 umbrella for $1,520. Harborview does not write umbrellas, so choosing Harborview would leave the client without an umbrella unless one is placed elsewhere; Ethan is asking a fourth carrier for a standalone umbrella quote.

## Ethan's Recommendation

Move the BOP and umbrella to Summit Ridge Insurance, with the equipment breakdown endorsement, because the roasters and packaging line are critical to the business and the deductible stays at $2,500.

| Line | Recommended option | Premium |
|---|---|---|
| BOP with equipment breakdown | Summit Ridge | $8,005 |
| Umbrella | Summit Ridge | $1,520 |
| Commercial auto | Trailhead Casualty (renew) | $3,300 |
| Workers' compensation | Pinecrest (renew) | $4,050 |
| **Total recommended program** | | **$16,875** |

Comparison: the expiring program was $15,620. Renewing everything as offered with Cascade would be $8,140 + $1,450 umbrella (Cascade's umbrella renewal quote is unchanged) + $3,300 + $4,050 = $16,940. The recommended program saves $65 compared with renewing as-is, keeps the $2,500 deductible, and keeps equipment breakdown coverage.

If Natalie chooses the lowest-cost option instead, Ethan must document in the file that she was told about the higher $5,000 deductible and the umbrella gap.
