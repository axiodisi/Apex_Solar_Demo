# Claim File — Brookside Coffee Roasters LLC, Water Damage, August 2026

Fictional sample document for demonstration purposes. Agency claim file and follow-up log. Insured: Brookside Coffee Roasters LLC (owner Natalie Brooks). Carrier: Cascade Mutual, BOP policy BOP-4471902. Account CSR: Hannah Kowalski. Producer: Ethan Saldana. Last updated October 16, 2026.

## Loss Summary

- Carrier claim number: CM-26-118204.
- Date of loss: weekend of August 22–23, 2026. Discovered Monday, August 24, 2026 at about 7:40 a.m. when staff opened the shop.
- Cause: a failed water supply line under a sink at Pemberton Yoga Studio, the upstairs tenant, leaked through the ceiling into Brookside's storage room and packaging area.
- Damage: green (unroasted) coffee inventory soaked, and the bag-sealing packaging machine shorted out. The café was closed from August 24 through September 1 for drying and cleanup.

## Reporting (FNOL)

- Natalie called the agency at 8:05 a.m. on August 24. Hannah confirmed no one was hurt and that the building manager had shut off the water.
- Because the estimated loss was over $25,000, Hannah reported the claim to Cascade Mutual the same day, at 9:20 a.m.
- Adjuster: Rob Delgado, Cascade Mutual, (800) 555-0193. Claim number and adjuster details were emailed to Natalie at 10:02 a.m.
- Natalie was told to photograph everything before cleanup, keep the damaged machine and a sample of the damaged inventory until the adjuster saw them, and save receipts for the drying company.
