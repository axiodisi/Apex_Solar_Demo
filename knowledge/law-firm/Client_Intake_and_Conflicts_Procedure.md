# Marlowe Kessler LLP — Client Intake & Conflict Check Procedure

Fictional sample document for demonstration purposes. Revised March 1, 2026. Owner: Office Administrator (Dana Whitfield).

## 1. Purpose

Every new matter at Marlowe Kessler LLP must clear intake and a conflict check before any attorney gives legal advice, reviews documents, or accepts a retainer. This applies to all three practice groups: Estate Planning, Business & Contracts, and Family Law.

## 2. Initial Inquiry

- Prospective clients may contact the firm by phone, the website form, or walk-in. The receptionist records the inquiry in Clio within 2 hours of first contact.
- Required intake fields: full legal name of the prospective client, all adverse or opposing parties, any related businesses or family members involved, the practice area, and a one-paragraph summary of the issue.
- Staff must not ask for or record details of the facts beyond the one-paragraph summary until the conflict check is cleared. This protects the firm if a conflict is later found.

## 3. Conflict Check

- The intake coordinator runs every name (client, adverse parties, related entities, spouses) through the Clio conflict search and the legacy matter spreadsheet for matters opened before 2019.
- Results are sent to the responsible attorney within 1 business day of the inquiry.
- A "potential conflict" hit must be reviewed by the Conflicts Partner, currently Andrea Kessler. Only the Conflicts Partner may clear a potential conflict or approve a written conflict waiver.
- If a conflict cannot be cleared, the coordinator sends the standard non-engagement letter (template NE-2) within 2 business days and does not refer the prospect to a specific outside attorney; instead, they provide the state bar's lawyer referral service information.

## 4. Free Consultations

- Estate Planning and Business & Contracts offer a free 30-minute initial consultation.
- Family Law consultations are $150 for 60 minutes. The $150 is credited toward the retainer if the client engages the firm within 14 days of the consultation.
- Consultations are booked only after the conflict check is cleared.

## 5. Engagement

- No work begins until the client has signed the engagement letter AND the retainer (if any) has cleared the trust account.
- Engagement letters must state: scope of representation, billing method (flat fee or hourly), the responsible attorney, and the client's right to terminate.
- Matters are opened in Clio with a matter number in the format YY-PRACTICE-#### (for example, 26-EST-0142).

## 6. Turnaround Targets

| Step | Target |
|---|---|
| Log inquiry in Clio | within 2 hours |
| Conflict results to attorney | 1 business day |
| Consultation offered | within 5 business days of clearance |
| Engagement letter sent after consult | within 1 business day |

## 7. Declined Matters

The firm does not accept criminal defense, personal injury, or immigration matters. Prospects in these areas receive the state bar's lawyer referral information and a non-engagement letter.
