# Marlowe Kessler LLP — Fee Schedule & Billing Policy

Fictional sample document for demonstration purposes. Effective January 1, 2026.

## 1. Hourly Rates

| Timekeeper | Hourly rate |
|---|---|
| Senior Partner | $425 |
| Partner | $375 |
| Associate (years 1–3) | $260 |
| Associate (years 4+) | $310 |
| Paralegal | $145 |
| Legal assistant | $95 |

Time is billed in 0.1-hour (6-minute) increments. Rates are reviewed every January; existing clients receive 30 days' written notice of any increase.

## 2. Flat-Fee Packages (Estate Planning)

| Package | Fee | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Essential Will Package | $1,250 | Will, financial power of attorney, healthcare directive |
| Revocable Living Trust — Individual | $2,950 | Trust, pour-over will, POAs, healthcare directive, deed transfer of one primary residence |
| Revocable Living Trust — Married Couple | $3,750 | Joint trust plus all of the above for both spouses |
| Trust Amendment | $650 | One amendment to a trust drafted by the firm |

Flat fees are earned in two stages: 50% at signing of the engagement letter and 50% at the document signing meeting. Additional real-estate deed transfers beyond the first are $275 each.

## 3. Retainers

- Family Law matters require a $5,000 retainer, deposited into the IOLTA trust account.
- Business litigation matters require a $7,500 retainer.
- Business transactional work (contracts, entity formation) generally requires no retainer for matters under $3,000 of estimated fees.
- When the trust balance falls below $1,500, the client is asked to replenish it back to the original retainer amount within 10 days. Work may pause if the replenishment is not received.

## 4. Invoices and Payment

- Invoices are issued on the 1st business day of each month through Clio and are due within 15 days.
- Accepted payment methods: ACH, credit card (3% card processing fee for amounts over $2,500), or check.
- Balances unpaid after 45 days accrue interest at 1% per month, as stated in the engagement letter.
- The firm does not bill for: the first 30-minute consultation (Estate Planning and Business only), internal conference time under 0.2 hours, or correcting the firm's own errors.

## 5. Costs Passed Through

Court filing fees, recording fees, process server fees, and expert fees are billed at cost with no markup. Photocopying is $0.15 per page for jobs over 100 pages; smaller jobs are not billed.

## 6. Billing Disputes

Clients who question an invoice should contact the Billing Manager, Luis Ortega, within 30 days. The responsible attorney reviews the dispute and responds in writing within 10 business days. Undisputed portions of the invoice remain due on the normal schedule.
