# Marlowe Kessler LLP — Firm Overview

Fictional sample document for demonstration purposes. Internal onboarding overview, updated January 2026.

## History

Marlowe Kessler LLP was founded in 2009 by Thomas Marlowe and Andrea Kessler, who left a regional firm to start a practice focused on owner-managed businesses and the families behind them. The firm began as a two-attorney business law practice, added Estate Planning in 2013, and added Family Law in 2018 when Senior Partner Gloria Reyes joined.

## Practice Groups

- **Business & Contracts** — entity formation, commercial contracts, asset and stock purchases, and business litigation (breach of contract, partnership disputes). Group leader: Thomas Marlowe.
- **Estate Planning** — wills, revocable trusts, powers of attorney, trust administration. Group leader: Samuel Okafor.
- **Family Law** — divorce, custody, support, prenuptial agreements. Group leader: Gloria Reyes.

## Office

One office, open 8:30 a.m. – 5:30 p.m. Monday through Friday. The main line is answered by the receptionist; after-hours calls go to voicemail and are returned the next business day. The firm closes on the holidays listed in the staff handbook.
