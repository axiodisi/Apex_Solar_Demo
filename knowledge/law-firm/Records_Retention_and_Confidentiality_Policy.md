# Marlowe Kessler LLP — Records Retention & Confidentiality Policy

Fictional sample document for demonstration purposes. Approved by the Management Committee, February 2026.

## 1. Retention Periods

| Record type | Retention after matter closes |
|---|---|
| General client files (business, family law) | 7 years |
| Estate planning files | Life of the client plus 7 years |
| Trust account records (IOLTA ledgers, bank statements) | 7 years |
| Files involving a minor | 7 years after the minor turns 18 |
| Conflict check records | Permanent |

Original wills and trusts are never destroyed by the firm. If the client cannot be located at the end of the retention period, originals are kept in the fire-rated vault.

## 2. Closing a Matter

- The responsible attorney sends a closing letter that states the matter is concluded and explains how long the file will be kept.
- Client-provided original documents are returned with the closing letter, by certified mail or in-person pickup.
- The file is marked closed in Clio within 5 business days of the closing letter.

## 3. Destruction

Files past their retention date are reviewed each January by the Office Administrator. Paper files are shredded by the firm's bonded shredding vendor, which provides a certificate of destruction. Electronic files are deleted from Clio and NetDocuments and the deletion is logged.

## 4. Confidentiality Rules for Staff

- Client matters are never discussed in public places, on personal social media, or with family members.
- Client documents may only be stored in NetDocuments or Clio. Personal Dropbox, Google Drive, or email accounts must never be used.
- Email to clients containing sensitive documents must use the NetDocuments secure share link rather than an attachment.
- Printing at home is not permitted.

## 5. Remote Work

- Attorneys and paralegals may work remotely up to 2 days per week with practice-group leader approval. Reception and the intake coordinator are in-office roles.
- Remote work requires the firm-issued laptop, the firm VPN, and a private workspace where calls cannot be overheard.
- Public Wi-Fi may only be used with the VPN connected.

## 6. Use of AI Tools

- Staff may use only firm-approved AI tools that keep client data private and do not train on it. The approved tool list is maintained by the Office Administrator.
- No client names, facts, or documents may be pasted into public AI chatbots.
- Any AI-assisted draft must be reviewed line by line by an attorney before it is sent to a client, court, or opposing counsel.

## 7. Data Breach Response

Any suspected loss of a device or unauthorized access must be reported to the Office Administrator and the Managing Partner, Thomas Marlowe, immediately and no later than 1 hour after discovery.
