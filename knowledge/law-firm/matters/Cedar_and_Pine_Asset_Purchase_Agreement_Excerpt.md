# Asset Purchase Agreement (Excerpt) — Cedar & Pine Bakery Co. / Harlan Mills Bakery

Fictional sample document for demonstration purposes. Matter 26-BUS-0057. Firm draft v4 dated May 22, 2026, prepared by Andrea Kessler for the Buyer, Cedar & Pine Bakery Co. Seller: Harlan Mills, sole owner of Harlan Mills Bakery. Purchase price: $640,000. This excerpt reproduces Article 8 (Indemnification), which is the firm's standard buyer-side indemnification clause for small business acquisitions.

## 8.1 Indemnification by Seller

Seller shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Buyer and its officers, members, and employees from and against any and all losses, damages, liabilities, costs, and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees ("Losses"), arising out of or relating to: (a) any breach of Seller's representations and warranties in Article 4; (b) any breach of Seller's covenants in this Agreement; (c) any Excluded Liability, including all debts, taxes, and obligations of the business arising before the Closing Date; and (d) any claim by an employee of the business relating to the period before the Closing Date.

## 8.2 Indemnification by Buyer

Buyer shall indemnify Seller from and against any Losses arising out of (a) any breach of Buyer's representations, warranties, or covenants, and (b) the operation of the business after the Closing Date.

## 8.3 Limitations

- Basket: Seller is not liable under Section 8.1(a) until total Losses exceed $10,000 (1.5625% of the purchase price), after which Seller is liable for all Losses from the first dollar (a "tipping" basket).
- Cap: Seller's total liability under Section 8.1(a) shall not exceed $160,000 (25% of the purchase price), except for fraud and breaches of the Fundamental Representations (organization, authority, title to assets, and taxes), which are capped at the full purchase price.
- Survival: General representations and warranties survive for 18 months after Closing. Fundamental Representations survive until 60 days after the applicable statute of limitations expires.

## 8.4 Holdback

Buyer shall hold back $64,000 (10% of the purchase price) from the amount paid at Closing as security for Seller's indemnification obligations. Any portion of the holdback not subject to a pending claim shall be released to Seller 18 months after the Closing Date.

## 8.5 Claims Procedure

A party seeking indemnification must give written notice describing the claim in reasonable detail within 30 days after learning of it. For third-party claims, the indemnifying party may assume the defense with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party within 15 days after notice.

## 8.6 Exclusive Remedy

Except for fraud and claims for injunctive relief, this Article 8 is the exclusive remedy of the parties for any breach of this Agreement after the Closing.
