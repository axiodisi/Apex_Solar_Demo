# Deposition of Karen Pruitt (Excerpt) — Henley Outdoor Supply LLC v. Redstone Logistics, Inc.

Fictional sample document for demonstration purposes. Case No. 2:26-cv-00418 (fictional). Deposition taken September 17, 2026. Witness: Karen Pruitt, Operations Manager, Redstone Logistics, Inc. Examining attorney: Thomas Marlowe for Plaintiff. Defending attorney: Brian Castellanos for Defendant. Excerpt from pages 41–47 of the transcript, prepared by Jordan Pike.

## Pages 41–43: The June 3, 2025 Meeting

Q. Ms. Pruitt, did you attend a meeting with Henley Outdoor Supply on June 3, 2025?
A. Yes. It was a planning call for the holiday season.

Q. Who else attended?
A. From Henley, Rachel Henley and their logistics coordinator, I think his name was Tyler. From our side, me and our account manager, Sophie Lang.

Q. What did Henley tell you about expected volume for the 2025 peak season?
A. They said they expected about double the order volume from the year before. They had a big wholesale deal and some promotions planned.

Q. Did you write that down?
A. I took notes in my notebook, and I sent a summary email to Sophie and to Paul Danner afterward.

Q. Paul Danner is Redstone's CEO?
A. Yes.

Q. What did your summary email say about staffing?
A. I said that if the volume came in at double, we would need about 14 additional seasonal pickers starting in October.

## Pages 44–46: Staffing Decisions

Q. Did Redstone hire 14 additional seasonal pickers for the 2025 peak season?
A. No.

Q. How many did it hire?
A. Six, I believe.

Q. Who made that decision?
A. Paul approved the seasonal budget. I asked for fourteen. The budget covered six.

Q. Did you tell anyone at Henley that you would only have six additional pickers?
MR. CASTELLANOS: Objection, form.
A. Not directly. No.

Q. Were you aware in November 2025 that orders were going out late?
A. Yes. We were behind most of November and into December.

## Page 47: Inventory Counts

Q. Redstone's agreement required monthly cycle counts of Henley's inventory, correct?
A. That's my understanding.

Q. Were cycle counts done in October, November, and December 2025?
A. October, yes. November and December, no. We didn't have the people.

Q. So the shortage of about 1,940 units wasn't reported to Henley until January?
A. That's correct. It came out in the January count.
