# Scheduling Order — Henley Outdoor Supply LLC v. Redstone Logistics, Inc.

Fictional sample document for demonstration purposes. United States District Court for the Western District of Lakeland (fictional). Case No. 2:26-cv-00418. Entered April 6, 2026 by the Hon. Elena Alvarez, United States District Judge.

## Order

Having reviewed the parties' Rule 26(f) report, the Court ORDERS that the following deadlines govern this case. These deadlines will not be extended except on a showing of good cause, and a request to extend must be filed before the deadline expires.

## Deadlines

| Event | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Motions to amend pleadings or join parties | June 1, 2026 |
| Completion of court-ordered mediation | November 20, 2026 |
| Close of fact discovery | January 15, 2027 |
| Plaintiff's expert disclosures | February 12, 2027 |
| Defendant's expert disclosures | March 12, 2027 |
| Close of expert discovery | April 16, 2027 |
| Dispositive motions (including summary judgment) | May 14, 2027 |
| Final pretrial conference | August 2, 2027 at 10:00 a.m. |
| Jury trial (estimated 5 days) | August 23, 2027 at 9:00 a.m. |

## Discovery Limits

- Each side may take no more than 8 depositions without leave of Court. Each deposition is limited to one day of 7 hours.
- Interrogatories are limited to 25 per party, including discrete subparts, as provided in Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 33.
- All discovery requests must be served early enough that responses are due before the close of fact discovery.

## Mediation

The parties shall complete mediation with a mutually agreed private mediator by November 20, 2026, and file a joint notice of the result within 7 days after the mediation. Party representatives with full settlement authority must attend in person.

## Discovery Disputes

Before filing any discovery motion, counsel must meet and confer in person or by video and then request a telephone conference with the Court. Discovery motions filed without a conference with the Court will be denied without prejudice.
