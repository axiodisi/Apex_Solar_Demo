# Standing Order for Civil Cases — Hon. Elena Alvarez

Fictional sample document for demonstration purposes. United States District Court for the Western District of Lakeland (fictional). Applies to all civil cases assigned to Judge Alvarez. Revised January 2026.

## Briefs and Page Limits

- Opening briefs on dispositive motions (including summary judgment): 25 pages.
- Opposition briefs: 25 pages.
- Reply briefs: 12 pages.
- All other motions: 15 pages for opening and opposition briefs, 8 pages for replies.
- Page limits exclude the caption, tables of contents and authorities, signature block, and exhibits.
- Text must be at least 12-point font, double-spaced, with footnotes at least 11-point. Footnotes may not be used to evade page limits.

## Summary Judgment

- Only one motion for summary judgment per side is allowed without leave of Court.
- Any motion for summary judgment must include a separate Statement of Undisputed Material Facts in numbered paragraphs, each supported by a citation to the record. The statement may not exceed 15 pages.

## Courtesy Copies

Paper courtesy copies are required for any filing (including exhibits) longer than 50 pages, delivered to chambers within 2 business days of electronic filing.

## Hearings and Continuances

- Motions are decided on the papers unless the Court sets a hearing.
- Requests for continuance of any deadline must be filed at least 7 days before the deadline and state the number of prior continuances.

## Settlement

The parties must notify chambers by email within 24 hours if the case settles in whole or in part.
