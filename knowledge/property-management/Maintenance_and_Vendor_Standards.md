# Larkspur Property Management — Maintenance Response & Vendor Standards

Fictional sample document for demonstration purposes. Internal operations standard for the maintenance team. Revised January 2026.

## 1. Priority Levels and Response Times

| Priority | Examples | First response | Resolution target |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 Emergency | Active leak, no A/C above 90°F, sewage backup, no power, unsecured door | Technician on site within 4 hours | Make safe the same day |
| P2 Urgent | Refrigerator not cooling, only toilet not working, water heater out | Contact within 4 business hours | 2 business days |
| P3 Routine | Dripping faucet, cabinet hinge, ceiling fan, garbage disposal | Contact within 1 business day | 5 business days |
| P4 Cosmetic | Paint touch-up, blinds, minor drywall | Contact within 2 business days | 10 business days or at turnover |

If a P1 A/C repair cannot be completed the same day, the resident is provided portable cooling units within 6 hours. If the home is still without A/C after 24 hours, Larkspur offers a hotel stipend of up to $150 per night.

## 2. Summer Readiness

Every property receives an A/C service (coil cleaning, refrigerant check, filter change) each April. Residents receive 48 hours' notice. HVAC filters are replaced every 90 days by the resident; Larkspur ships filters to residents quarterly at no charge.

## 3. Vendor Requirements

All vendors must have, before receiving any work order:
- An active contractor license where the trade requires one (for example, state contractor licenses for plumbing, electrical, HVAC, and roofing).
- General liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence, naming Larkspur Property Management as additional insured.
- Workers' compensation coverage, or a signed sole-proprietor exemption.
- A signed W-9 and vendor agreement.

Certificates of insurance are tracked in AppFolio and vendors are suspended automatically when a certificate expires.

## 4. Pricing and Markups

- Vendor invoices are passed to owners at cost. Larkspur does not add markups to vendor invoices.
- In-house maintenance technician labor is billed at $65 per hour with a 1-hour minimum, and $95 per hour after hours or on weekends.
- Trip charges for no-access (resident not home despite notice) are $45 and are charged to the resident.

## 5. Make-Ready (Turnover) Standard

Vacant homes must be rent-ready within 7 calendar days of move-out. The checklist includes: professional cleaning, rekeying all exterior locks, replacing HVAC filter and smoke detector batteries, testing all appliances, and a photo walkthrough of every room uploaded to AppFolio.

## 6. Quality Checks

The maintenance coordinator calls 1 in 5 residents after a completed work order to confirm the issue is resolved. Any vendor with three callbacks for the same issue in 90 days is placed on review.
