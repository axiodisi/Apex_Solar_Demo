# Larkspur Property Management — Owner Management Agreement Summary

Fictional sample document for demonstration purposes. Plain-language summary of the standard Owner Management Agreement, 2026 edition. The signed agreement controls if anything here differs.

## 1. Management Fees

| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| Monthly management fee (single-family and condos) | 8% of rent collected |
| Monthly management fee (portfolios of 5+ doors) | 7% of rent collected |
| Leasing fee (new resident placement) | 50% of one month's rent |
| Lease renewal fee | $250 |
| Setup fee (new property onboarding) | $199, one time |
| Eviction coordination | $300 plus attorney and court costs |

No management fee is charged on months when the property is vacant.

## 2. Owner Statements and Payouts

- Owner statements are published in the Owner Portal on the 10th of each month.
- Owner distributions are sent by ACH on the 12th of each month (or the next business day).
- Larkspur holds an operating reserve of $300 per property in the trust account to cover routine repairs.

## 3. Repair Approval Limits

- Larkspur may approve any single repair up to $500 without contacting the owner.
- Repairs between $500 and $2,500 require owner approval by email or Owner Portal. If the owner does not respond within 48 hours, Larkspur will call the owner once more before proceeding only if the repair affects habitability.
- Repairs over $2,500 require two written bids and owner approval, except emergencies.
- Emergencies that threaten health, safety, or the property (for example, a burst pipe or an A/C failure during summer) may be handled immediately regardless of cost, with the owner notified within 24 hours.

## 4. Resident Screening Standards

- Minimum gross household income of 3 times the monthly rent.
- No evictions in the past 5 years and no unpaid balances owed to a prior landlord.
- Credit is reviewed but there is no single minimum score; applicants under 600 may qualify with an additional deposit of half a month's rent.
- All adult occupants (18+) must apply. Screening criteria are applied identically to every applicant in compliance with fair housing laws.
- Larkspur does not use blanket criminal-history bans. A conviction is reviewed individually by the Leasing Manager, considering the nature of the offense and how long ago it occurred.
- Whenever information in a screening report contributes to a denial, a co-signer requirement, or an additional deposit, Larkspur sends the applicant an adverse action notice (template AA-1) by email within 3 business days. The notice names the screening company that supplied the report.
- The application fee is $55 per adult and is non-refundable once screening has been run.

## 5. Leasing Guarantee

If a resident placed by Larkspur is evicted for non-payment within the first 6 months of the lease, the next resident is placed with no leasing fee.

## 6. Ending the Agreement

Either party may terminate with 30 days' written notice after the initial 12-month term. If the owner terminates during the initial term, a cancellation fee of $500 applies. Larkspur transfers all leases, deposits, and records to the owner or new manager within 10 business days of termination.
