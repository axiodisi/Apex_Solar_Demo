# Larkspur Property Management — Resident Handbook

Fictional sample document for demonstration purposes. Applies to all residential properties managed by Larkspur. Version 2026.1.

## 1. Rent Payment

- Rent is due on the 1st of each month. Payments are made through the Resident Portal (ACH free; debit card $2.95 fee; credit card 2.9% fee).
- A grace period runs through 11:59 p.m. on the 3rd. Starting on the 4th, a late fee of $75 is charged, plus $10 per day until paid, capped at $175 per month.
- Returned payments (NSF) incur a $35 fee. After two returned payments in 12 months, the resident must pay by certified funds or money order for the remainder of the lease.

## 2. Maintenance Requests

- Submit all non-emergency requests through the Resident Portal with photos. Phone requests are accepted only for emergencies.
- Emergencies (no heat in winter, no A/C when outside temperature is above 90°F, active water leak, sewage backup, gas smell, no electricity, broken exterior door lock) should be called in to the 24/7 line at (800) 555-0148. For a gas smell, leave the home and call the gas utility first.
- By entering a maintenance request, residents give permission for a technician to enter during business hours (8 a.m. – 5 p.m., Monday – Friday) unless the request says otherwise.

## 3. Pets

- Maximum of 2 pets per home. Dogs on the restricted breed list and any animal over 80 pounds are not permitted.
- One-time pet fee: $300 per pet (non-refundable). Monthly pet rent: $35 per pet.
- Assistance animals and service animals are not pets: no pet fee, pet rent, or breed/weight restriction applies. Requests are reviewed under our reasonable-accommodation process within 10 business days.
- Unauthorized pets result in a $250 charge plus back pet rent from the date the pet was first observed.

## 4. Move-Out and Security Deposit

- Residents must give written notice to vacate at least 30 days before the end of the lease. Notice is submitted through the Resident Portal.
- A move-out inspection is completed within 2 business days of key return. Residents are welcome to attend.
- The itemized deposit statement and any refund are mailed within 14 business days of move-out to the forwarding address on file.
- Standard cleaning charge if the home is not left clean: $225 (homes under 1,500 sq ft) or $325 (1,500 sq ft and over). Carpet cleaning is charged only if carpets are stained or soiled beyond normal wear.

## 5. Breaking a Lease Early

Residents who need to leave before the lease ends may choose the Early Termination Option: 60 days' written notice plus an early termination fee equal to one month's rent. Rent is due through the 60-day notice period. Active-duty military residents with PCS or deployment orders may terminate with 30 days' notice and no fee.

## 6. Community Rules

- Quiet hours: 10 p.m. – 7 a.m.
- Vehicles must be operable and currently registered. Boats, RVs, and trailers may not be parked on the property for more than 24 hours.
- Satellite dishes may not be attached to the roof. Smoke detectors must never be disabled; residents replace batteries, Larkspur replaces failed units.
