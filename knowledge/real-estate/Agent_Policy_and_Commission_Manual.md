# Bellwether Realty Group — Agent Policy & Commission Manual

Fictional sample document for demonstration purposes. For all licensed agents affiliated with Bellwether Realty Group. Edition 2026.

## 1. Commission Splits

| Plan | Agent / Brokerage split | Cap |
|---|---|---|
| New Agent Plan (first 12 months) | 60 / 40 | No cap |
| Standard Plan | 75 / 25 | Brokerage share capped at $18,000 per anniversary year |
| Team Plan | 80 / 20 on team-generated deals | Team leader pays the brokerage share |

After an agent reaches the cap, they pay a $195 transaction fee per closed deal for the rest of the anniversary year. Every agent pays a $395 transaction fee per closing before reaching the cap, plus $49 per month for technology (CRM, e-signature, transaction management).

## 2. Referral Fees

- Outbound referrals to agents at other brokerages: the referral agreement must be signed before the client is introduced. Typical referral fee is 25% of the receiving agent's side.
- Inbound referral fees are paid by the brokerage, not by the agent personally, and only after the transaction has closed and funded.

## 3. Buyer Representation

- A signed written buyer representation agreement is required before an agent tours any home with a buyer, including new construction and open houses attended together.
- The agreement must state the amount or rate of compensation the agent will receive (a specific dollar amount or percentage, never "whatever the seller offers") and that compensation is negotiable.
- Agents may not receive compensation from any source that exceeds the amount in the buyer agreement.

## 4. Listing Standards

- Every listing gets professional photos (minimum 25 images), a floor plan, and a 3D tour for homes listed over $500,000. The brokerage pays for photos on the first 3 listings of each new agent.
- Listings must be entered in the MLS within 1 business day of the listing agreement's start date, unless the seller signs the office exclusive form.
- Coming-soon listings may not be shown to buyers until they go active.
- Price reductions require a signed amendment from all sellers before the MLS is updated.

## 5. Earnest Money

- Earnest money must be delivered to the title or escrow company within 3 business days of contract acceptance, unless the contract says otherwise.
- Agents may never hold earnest money checks overnight in their car or home. Checks are dropped at the office safe if the title company is closed.

## 6. Advertising Rules

All advertising (including social media posts) must show the brokerage name, Bellwether Realty Group, at least as prominently as the agent's or team's name. Team names may not include the words "realty," "brokerage," or "real estate company."

## 7. Escalations

Agents must contact the Managing Broker, Renee Castellano, immediately for: any claim of misrepresentation, earnest money disputes, inspection disputes that may cancel a contract, or any fair housing concern. Agents may not give legal or tax advice; refer clients to an attorney or CPA.
