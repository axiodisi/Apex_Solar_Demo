# Bellwether Realty Group — Buyer Transaction Timeline & Checklist

Fictional sample document for demonstration purposes. Standard timeline agents walk buyers through after an offer is accepted. Dates are counted from contract acceptance unless noted. Always follow the dates in the signed purchase contract if they differ.

## 1. Day 0 – 3: Contract Accepted

- Send the fully signed contract to the buyer, the lender, and the title/escrow company the same day.
- Earnest money delivered to title within 3 business days.
- Buyer submits the loan application to their lender, if not already done. The lender must provide a Loan Estimate within 3 business days of receiving the application. Agents should encourage buyers to compare Loan Estimates from more than one lender.

## 2. Day 1 – 10: Inspection Period

- Book the home inspection within 2 days of acceptance. Recommended add-ons: sewer scope for homes built before 1980, roof inspection for roofs over 15 years old, and termite inspection where required by the lender or the contract.
- The agent attends the last 30 minutes of the inspection to hear the inspector's summary.
- Repair requests or cancellation notices must be delivered in writing before the inspection period ends. Missing this date usually means the buyer accepts the home as-is.

## 3. Day 10 – 21: Appraisal and Insurance

- The lender orders the appraisal. If the appraisal comes in below the contract price, the agent schedules a call with the buyer within 24 hours to review options: renegotiate, pay the difference, or cancel if the contract has an appraisal contingency.
- The buyer must get homeowners insurance quotes in the first week. If the home is in a flood zone, the lender will require flood insurance; buyers should get a flood quote early because it can significantly change the monthly payment.

## 4. Day 21 – 27: Final Loan Approval

- Buyers must not open new credit, change jobs, or make large deposits or purchases without talking to the lender first.
- The buyer must receive the Closing Disclosure at least 3 business days before closing. Agents review it with the buyer side by side with the latest Loan Estimate and ask the lender about any differences.

## 5. Day 28 – 30: Final Walkthrough and Closing

- Final walkthrough within 24 hours before closing. Confirm agreed repairs are done (ask for receipts) and that included appliances and fixtures are present.
- Wire fraud warning: buyers must call the title company at a phone number they already know (from the contract or the title company's website, not from an email) to confirm wiring instructions. Bellwether agents never send wiring instructions.
- Bring a government-issued photo ID to closing.

## 6. After Closing

- Agent delivers the closing gift within 7 days and schedules a check-in call at 30 days.
- Reminder to the buyer: keep the Closing Disclosure and all closing documents permanently with their property records.
