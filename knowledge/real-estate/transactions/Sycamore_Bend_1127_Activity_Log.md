Fictional sample document for demonstration purposes. Communication and activity log from the transaction management system. Buyer's agent: Leah Vance. Transaction coordinator: Bianca Sorensen. Buyers: Nathaniel and Grace Obi.

- Sat, Sep 26 — Second showing of 1127 Sycamore Bend Lane. Grace asked about the age of the roof; listing disclosures show it was replaced in 2009.
- Mon, Sep 28 — Offer submitted at $485,000 with $7,500 earnest money.
- Tue, Sep 29 — Sellers accepted. Bianca sent the signed contract to the buyers, Teresa Yuen at Maple Federal Credit Union, and Will Hartman at Keystone Title the same day.
