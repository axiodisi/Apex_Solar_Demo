# Contract Terms and Amendments — 1127 Sycamore Bend Lane

Fictional sample document for demonstration purposes. Abstract of the signed residential purchase contract (accepted September 29, 2026) and its amendments, prepared by transaction coordinator Bianca Sorensen for buyer's agent Leah Vance. Buyers: Nathaniel and Grace Obi. Sellers: Harold and June Pratt. The signed contract and amendments control if anything here differs.

## Purchase Contract — Key Terms

- Price: $485,000 (later changed by Amendment #2). Earnest money: $7,500, held by Keystone Title & Escrow.
- Financing contingency: conventional loan of 90% of the purchase price or appraised value, whichever is less. Final loan approval due by October 23, 2026.
- Appraisal contingency: if the appraisal is below the price, the buyers may cancel with a refund of earnest money, or the parties may renegotiate, by written notice by October 20, 2026.
- Inspection contingency: 10 days after acceptance (through October 9, 2026). The buyers may cancel or submit a written repair request.
- Seller concessions: the sellers pay up to $4,000 of the buyers' closing costs and the buyer's agent compensation of $11,500.
- Home warranty: sellers pay for a one-year home warranty up to $550, ordered by the buyers' agent.
- Included items: refrigerator, range, dishwasher, microwave, garage door openers and remotes, window coverings, and the backyard shed. Excluded: the washer and dryer, and the dining room chandelier (sellers will replace it with a standard light fixture before closing).
- Possession: at closing, upon funding.
- Title insurance: owner's policy paid by sellers.

## Amendment #1 — Inspection Response (signed by all parties October 9, 2026)

Inspection by Jordan Ames, ClearView Home Inspections, on Friday, October 2, 2026, with sewer scope and roof inspection add-ons (home built 1974; roof 17 years old). Leah attended the last 30 minutes.

Seller credit for inspection items: $1,500 at closing, in addition to the $4,000 closing-cost credit. Total seller credits: $5,500. The lender confirmed on October 12 that total credits are within the loan program's limit.

## Amendment #2 — Appraisal Gap (signed by all parties October 16, 2026)

The appraisal (dated October 14, 2026) came in at $478,000, $7,000 below the $485,000 price. Leah called the Obis on October 15, within 24 hours of receiving the appraisal, and reviewed their options: renegotiate, pay the difference, or cancel under the appraisal contingency.

Agreed terms:
- Price reduced by $3,500, from $485,000 to $481,500.
- The buyers pay the remaining $3,500 above the appraised value in cash at closing.
- The buyers waive the appraisal contingency.
- All other terms unchanged.

The loan amount is now $430,200 (90% of $478,000).
